# Settings form

Meridian is a scheduling tool sold to dental practices. This is the screen where
the practice owner manages their own account, and they reach it perhaps twice a
year, so nothing about it can rely on the reader remembering how it worked last
time. It covers four things. Their profile, which is a display name, an email
address, a practice name and a time zone. Which of six event types email them,
each on or off independently. Their billing plan, which is one of three tiers,
the card on file, and the next charge date. And closing the account, which is
permanent, removes the practice's data after thirty days, and must be typed to
confirm. The email address must be checked as a real address, the display name
must be between two and sixty characters, and both must report what is wrong
with them without the owner losing anything else they typed.

Build this in the WebJs app in this directory.
